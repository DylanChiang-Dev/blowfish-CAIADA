## 🚀 2026 Shandong AI Industry Study Tour

We are thrilled to announce the **2026 Shandong AI Industry Study Tour**, hosted by the **Chinese AI Application Development Association (CAIADA)**. 

This tour is designed primarily for outstanding youth students and entrepreneurs to explore leading AI, smart manufacturing, and e-commerce industries in China. We will visit three major hub cities in Shandong: **Qingdao, Zibo, and Jinan**.

---

### 📅 Quick Info

*   **Official Itinerary**: August 16, 2026 (Sun) - August 22, 2026 (Sat) (7 Days & 6 Nights)
*   **Special Benefit (Extended Stay)**: To support deep exploration and individual academic exchanges, the host in Shandong offers a special extended stay package up to **August 30, 2026 (Sun)**. The local hosts will continue to provide support during the extended period.
*   **Tour Cost**: Approx. **TWD 19,000** (Covers round-trip flight Taipei-Qingdao, insurance, and administrative fees).
*   **Host Coverage**: All local transport, hotel accommodation, dining, company visit bookings, and field executions within Shandong are **fully covered by the local hosts**.
